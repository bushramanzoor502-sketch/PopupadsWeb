1. Data safety answers

Google Play defines "collected" as data transmitted off the user's device, and "shared" as data transferred to a third party. ClearGuard transmits nothing.

2. Data processed on the device only

Not "collected" under Play's definition, but listed here for transparency:

  • App activity: package name, event type and time of window-change events during user-started sessions.
  • Installed apps: launcher-visible apps and their names, icons, install source and selected abilities, shown in the Visible apps list and the Permissions checkup.
  • User-entered answers attached to investigations (what was seen, approximate time, follow-up).

All of it stays on the phone, is excluded from backup and device transfer, and follows the retention described in the privacy policy.

4. Permissions policy

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: optional, requested in context from the Access screen, used only for ClearGuard's own session controls, saved-popup notes and the opt-in reminder.
  • REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES: opens Android's own uninstall confirmation for an app the user picked; the app deletes nothing itself.
  • No QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: narrow <queries> for launcher, home, chooser and web-browser intents only.
  • No SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW, foreground service, usage access, location or other sensitive permissions.

Full detail: permissions explained.

5. Deceptive behavior and claims

  • Results are always worded as "evidence to review, not proof". The app shows "Possible source", "Ambiguous" or "Source unavailable" with a reason.
  • No accuracy percentages, no "your phone is safe", and no "malware", "adware" or "harmful" labels on apps.
  • Not presented as an antivirus, security scanner or ad blocker. It does not block or interfere with ads in other apps.
  • Uninstall, disable and change permissions are offered as equal options in Android's App info; ClearGuard never removes apps itself and does not push removal.
  • Limits are stated openly: many overlays and in-page ads report nothing, and popups before a session are not seen.
  • No imitation of system warnings or notifications.

6. Families policy and target audience

ClearGuard is not designed for or directed at children, and is not submitted to the Designed for Families programme. The intended target audience is adults. confirm the age group selected in Play Console (for example 18+); if a group under 13 is ever included, Families requirements apply

7. Other declarations

  • Ads: the app contains no ads.
  • App access: no login is required. Reviewers can use "Try a test popup" during a session to see a full result without real adware.
  • In-app purchases: none in the app (no billing library).
  • Google Play In-App Review: may request Google's rating prompt once after a positive outcome; no data about investigations is sent.
