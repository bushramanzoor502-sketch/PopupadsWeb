Delete your ClearGuard data

ClearGuard keeps everything on your phone and has no account. You delete your data yourself, inside the app or by uninstalling it. There is nothing stored on our side, so no request to us is needed.

1. No account, no server data

ClearGuard has no user accounts and no internet permission. It never sends data to UtilEdge or anyone else, so we hold no data about you and there is nothing to request deletion of from us. All ClearGuard data lives on your phone, and the steps below remove it.

2. Delete a single investigation or session

Open ClearGuard and go to History. Then use either way:

From the History list

  1. Tap the delete (bin) button on the record, or swipe the record toward the start of the line (to the left in left to right languages).
  2. A "Deleted · Undo" bar appears for 5 seconds. Tap Undo to keep the record. Otherwise it is removed for good when the 5 seconds end, or straight away when you leave History.

From the record itself

  1. Open the investigation or session and tap Delete investigation (or Delete session).
  2. Confirm.

Either way, its evidence, candidates, corrections and your answers are removed from the phone.

3. Delete all history

  1. Open ClearGuard → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete all history and confirm.

Every saved investigation and session on the phone is removed. Your settings and consent stay as they are.

4. Reset ClearGuard

  1. Open ClearGuard → Settings.
  2. Tap Reset ClearGuard and confirm.

Observation stops, and all saved investigations and sessions, the last App info record, settings and the local encryption keys are removed. Your agreement to the disclosure is kept, and Android's Accessibility setting for ClearGuard stays on until you turn it off there (the reset screen offers a button to open it), so you can start a session again right away. To take back your agreement, use Withdraw consent in Access.

5. Automatic deletion

  • Saved investigations and sessions are deleted automatically after 7 days by default. Change this in Settings → Keep investigations to 1, 7 or 30 days.
  • If saved history would grow past 20 MB, the oldest records are deleted first.
  • Recent window events are kept in memory only, for about two minutes, and are cleared when a session is paused or stopped, the screen turns off, or access is lost.

6. Stop ClearGuard observing

  • Withdraw consent: ClearGuard → Access → Withdraw consent. Any running session stops and nothing is observed until you agree again.
  • Turn off the service: Android Settings → Accessibility → ClearGuard → off.
  • Automatic detection: ClearGuard Settings → Detect popups automatically → off, so only popups you mark are saved.

7. Uninstall

Uninstall ClearGuard from Android Settings → Apps → ClearGuard → Uninstall, or by long pressing its icon. Android deletes all of ClearGuard's data and its encryption keys from the phone. ClearGuard is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer, so no copy is restored if you reinstall or move to a new phone.

8. What these steps do not delete

  • Reports you saved with "Save report" (text or PDF) are files in the place you chose. Delete them there, for example in your Files app or cloud drive.
  • Your Google Play account, install history and any rating or review you left are held by Google. Manage them in Google Play or your Google Account.
  • Deletion is done by the app and Android in the normal way; ClearGuard does not promise forensic erasure of flash storage.
