4. What ClearGuard's results mean

A result is evidence to review, not proof. ClearGuard shows which app reported a window change around the time a popup appeared. That an app reported a window change does not prove it showed an ad, contains adware, or is harmful.

  • Many popups do not reveal their source. Overlays drawn over other apps and ads inside an app or web page often report nothing to Android, so ClearGuard may show "Source unavailable".
  • A result may be ambiguous (several apps) or may name an app that only coincidentally changed a window, for example when you opened a link, shared, signed in or paid in another app.
  • ClearGuard cannot see popups that happened before a session started, and behaviour differs between phone makers and Android versions.
  • ClearGuard is not an antivirus, security scanner or ad blocker, and does not tell you whether your phone is safe.
